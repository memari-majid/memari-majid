# Majid Memari, Ph.D., M.B.A.

I build and evaluate applied AI systems, then turn that work into practical learning experiences. I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at Utah Valley University, an NVIDIA DLI Certified Instructor for **Building Agentic AI Applications with LLMs**, and an NVIDIA DLI University Ambassador.

My work spans **LLMs and RAG, multi-agent systems, model evaluation, computer vision, and AI education**.

[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LQI4T24AAAAJ&hl=en) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5654-4996) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/majid-memari/) · [NVIDIA instructor profile](https://www.nvidia.com/en-eu/training/instructor-directory/bio/?instructorId=003Vv00000J9hZLIAZ)

## Selected public projects

| Project | Hands-on work |
| --- | --- |
| [Willow](https://github.com/memari-majid/willow) | AI reflection-companion prototype built with Next.js and TypeScript. My contributions include a temporary no-login demo and chat-stability fixes; the project combines streamed responses, retrieval, memory, and safety checks. |
| [VirtualTA](https://github.com/memari-majid/VirtualTA) | Python/Streamlit RAG prototype for querying LLM-course materials with LangChain, HuggingFace embeddings, PostgreSQL/pgvector, and local Ollama models. An independent educational project. |
| [Wind-turbine blade defect detection](https://github.com/memari-majid/Wind-Turbine-Blade-Defect-Detection-with-YOLO-Models) | Computer-vision research exploring YOLO-based detection of small blade defects, image augmentation, and training strategies. |
| [GuardianSense](https://github.com/memari-majid/GuardianSense) | Multimodal assistive-AI research prototype with synthetic text, image, audio, video, and physiological-data generation. |
| [Centering research notebooks](https://github.com/memari-majid/jhu_centering) | Collaborative meditation-study work: survey and sensor preprocessing, research notebooks, and statistical analyses. |

These repositories include research and educational prototypes. Willow and GuardianSense are not clinically validated medical systems.

## Research and teaching projects

- **DataGovAI:** Project lead on a multi-agent RAG research system for government records-retention compliance with the Utah Office of Data Privacy.
- **AI-ClassSims:** Co-PI and technical contributor on multi-agent classroom simulation with real-time feedback for teacher preparation.
- **CareWise:** Development of an AI caregiver-support research prototype.
- **Educational LLM assistants:** Workshop and course tooling, source-grounded answers, evaluation cases, citation checks, and failure analysis.

Some research implementations are private. Publications and research details are available on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=LQI4T24AAAAJ&hl=en).
